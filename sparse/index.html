        Adjust C and γ

        Smugglers hide in the middle of Imperial patrols, so no straight line works. Tune an RBF SVM and compare training with held-out accuracy.

        RBF SVM, refitted on this page for every setting
          similarity between two ships: 1 when identical, near 0 when far apart.
          larger ⇒ similarity fades faster ⇒ each ship influences only a small bubble.
          larger ⇒ training mistakes cost more ⇒ the boundary bends to fit them.

          What to look for. Underfit: a blunt boundary, both accuracies low. Overfit: tiny islands around single ships, training accuracy near 100% but held-out accuracy drops.
