The Widest Corridor

          In plain words. All three lines classify every known ship correctly, so accuracy can’t choose between them. The margin can: line C leaves the widest empty corridor. A ship whose readings wobble a little is still on the right side.

          Show why a wide gap helps

          New ships are not copies of the old ones; their readings differ a bit. If a line passes within 0.27 units of a known Rebel, a Rebel ship with slightly different readings can fall on the Imperial side. A line 0.75 units from every known ship tolerates almost three times as much wobble.

          This is an intuition, not a guarantee: a wide margin tends to generalize well, but it can’t promise that every future ship is classified correctly.

          Notice also that the margin depends only on the nearest ships. Move a far-away ship a little and the margin doesn’t change. That idea returns in scene 6.
