How Tree Ensembles Learn

Four ways to combine small trees, plus the recipe behind three of them, all trained on the same 500 Titanic passengers. Step through the rounds one tree at a time. Each round shows the new tree, the rule it learned, and what it changed for the passenger you are following.

Two ways to build a committee of trees

A random forest grows many deep-ish trees independently, each on a different bootstrap sample and with a random subset of features at each split, then lets them vote. The trees never see each other.

Boosting grows trees one after another. Each new tree is a weak learner fitted to whatever the trees before it still get wrong, and the model is their weighted sum . No single shallow tree is accurate. Their sum is.

Forward stagewise additive modeling is the recipe all three boosting tabs follow. It never revisits old trees. At round it adds the single tree and step size that lower the training loss the most, and keeps everything else fixed:

Choosing the loss gives a specific method: squared loss gives least squares boosting, exponential loss gives AdaBoost, and logistic loss gives LogitBoost.

The method on this tab

bootstrap sample for tree m
running vote
  • Bagging trains each tree on its own bootstrap sample. About 63% of passengers appear at least once, some two or three times. The rest are out of bag for that tree.
  • Random forest adds one more source of variety. Each split may only choose from 2 of the 4 features, drawn at random. The tree card lists both candidates and the best split each one offered. A strong feature such as sex cannot win every root.
  • The trees are independent. Tree uses its own seed, so it is the same tree whether it is grown first, last or on a different machine. The round number only sets the playback order.
  • Each tree is grown by Gini impurity on its sample, using bootstrap counts as weights. Each leaf votes for its majority class. A tied vote counts as “died”.

  • The chart under “Line search” plots the training loss for every step size along the new tree. The dot is the step the method took. The ring is the exact minimizer. They coincide for least squares and AdaBoost. LogitBoost uses a fixed instead of searching, so its dot can sit beside the ring.
  • Earlier trees are frozen. Stagewise means each stage is solved once and never revisited. That is why the training loss can only go down, or stay flat, from one round to the next when the line search is exact.

  • With exponential loss, the stagewise step separates into two parts: fit a classifier to reweighted passengers, then pick in closed form. Weights are shown times 500, so an average passenger has weight 1.
  • Each tree is grown by weighted Gini, and each leaf outputs the weighted majority label, (survived) or (died).
  • After reweighting, the passengers this tree got wrong hold exactly half the total weight. The tree that was just added would score on the new weights, so the next tree is pushed to fix something else.
